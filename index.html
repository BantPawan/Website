01M.Tech research · 2026

GraphRAGXAI

Explainable retrieval for rare-disease candidate ranking.

PythonKnowledge graphsRAGNeo4j

THE PROBLEM

How can a system connect clinical findings to candidate diagnoses while keeping its generated explanation consistent with the evidence?

MY APPROACH

Combined a biomedical knowledge graph with retrieval and an LLM. Added a ranking safeguard so the narrative cannot silently change the computed order of candidates. Compared alternatives and revised the design based on evaluation.

Research prototype for candidate ranking; not a clinical diagnostic tool.
Read the evaluation snapshot

My resume reports a top-5 result of 77% across 70 patient cases, using a graph of 106,658 entities and 1.55 million relationships. These are project evaluation results, not evidence of clinical readiness.

03M.Tech research · 2025

Understanding job
automation risk.

Compared regression models on a 3,000-record synthetic labour-market dataset. Used feature selection, cross-validation and SHAP to explore what drove the predictions.

AdaBoostScikit-learnSHAP
What the results mean

The strongest model in my comparison was AdaBoost, with a reported R² of 0.871. Because the data is synthetic, this is a modelling exercise rather than a validated forecast of actual employment outcomes.
